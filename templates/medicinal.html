Medicinal Plants Guidance

Simple tips to grow healthy and useful medicinal plants.

🌿

❶ Light

☀️🌱
  • Provide sufficient sunlight according to the plant variety.
  • Most medicinal plants need 4–6 hours of sunlight daily.

❷ Watering

💧🌱
  • Keep the soil slightly moist but avoid excessive watering.
  • Water in the morning for most plants.

❸ Soil

🌱
  • Use organic, nutrient-rich and well-draining soil.
  • Avoid heavy soil that retains too much water.

❹ Fertilizer

🌿
  • Use organic fertilizer or compost during the growing season.
  • Do not use excessive fertilizer.

❺ Temperature

🌡️
  • Keep plants in moderate temperature.
  • Protect them from extreme cold, frost and strong hot winds.

❻ Cleaning

🍃
  • Wipe dust from leaves regularly.
  • Remove yellow, dry or damaged leaves.

❼ Pruning & Harvesting

✂️🌿
  • Prune regularly to promote healthy growth.
  • Harvest leaves or parts properly without damaging the plant.

❽ General Care

🌱🤲
  • Avoid chemical pesticides when possible.
  • Keep plants clean and healthy.
  • Follow proper guidance before using plants medicinally.
