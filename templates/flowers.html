Flower Plants Guidance

Simple care tips to keep your flower plants healthy, colorful and blooming.

🌹

❶ Light

☀️🌸
  • Most flowering plants need 4–6 hours of sunlight daily.
  • Provide morning sunlight.
  • Avoid strong afternoon sunlight for delicate flowers.

❷ Watering

💧🌸
  • Water regularly according to the plant's needs.
  • Keep soil moist but avoid waterlogging.
  • Water early morning or late evening.

❸ Soil

🌱
  • Use fertile, well-draining soil.
  • Good drainage helps prevent root rot.
  • Mix compost for better growth.

❹ Fertilizer

🌿
  • Use flowering-plant fertilizer.
  • Apply fertilizer every 2–4 weeks.
  • Do not use excessive fertilizer.

❺ Temperature

🌡️
  • Most flower plants grow well in moderate temperatures.
  • Protect from extreme cold, frost and hot winds.

❻ Pruning

✂️🌸
  • Remove dried flowers regularly.
  • Prune weak or damaged branches.
  • Pruning encourages healthy flowering.

❼ Pest Control

🔍🍃
  • Check plants regularly for pests and diseases.
  • Remove affected leaves or parts.

❽ Support & Care

🌸🪴
  • Provide support to tall plants.
  • Mulch around the plant to retain moisture.
  • Keep the area clean and weed-free.
