Fruit Plants Guidance

Simple tips to grow healthy and productive fruit plants.

🍊

❶ Light

☀️🌱
  • Fruit plants generally require good sunlight.
  • Provide at least 6–8 hours of sunlight daily.

❷ Watering

💧🪴
  • Water deeply when the soil begins to dry.
  • Keep soil moist but avoid overwatering.
  • Ensure proper drainage.

❸ Soil

🌱
  • Use fertile soil rich in organic matter.
  • Good drainage is essential for healthy roots.

❹ Fertilizer

🌿
  • Apply suitable organic or fruit-plant fertilizer.
  • Use balanced fertilizer for healthy growth.
  • Do not over-fertilize.

❺ Temperature

🌡️
  • Fruit plants grow best in moderate temperatures.
  • Protect plants from extreme weather.

❻ Pruning

✂️🌿
  • Prune dry, weak or diseased branches.
  • Pruning improves air circulation and fruit production.

❼ Pest Control

🔍🍃
  • Check plants regularly for pests and diseases.
  • Remove affected leaves or parts immediately.

❽ Support & Care

🌱🪵
  • Provide support to young plants when necessary.
  • Mulch around plants to retain moisture.
  • Keep the area free from weeds.
