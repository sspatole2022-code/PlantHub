OUR NURSERY

Welcome to Kanifnath PlantHub Nursery, a place where nature, quality, and passion come together to create beautiful and healthy green spaces. Located in Pune, Maharashtra, our nursery is committed to providing a wide collection of healthy, fresh, and carefully nurtured plants for every type of space.

Our nursery offers a diverse range of plants, including indoor plants, flowering plants, outdoor plants, ornamental plants, garden plants, and greenery for landscaping. Each plant is grown and maintained with proper care to ensure good health, strong growth, and long-lasting beauty.

Care for Your Plants

Small efforts, healthy plants, better tomorrow.

Water Properly

Water your plants regularly and give them enough water to keep the soil moist and healthy.

Plant Growth

Provide essential nutrients regularly to help your plants grow stronger, healthier and greener.

Give Enough Sunlight

Place your plants where they can receive the right amount of sunlight for healthy growth.

Keep Plants Healthy

Remove dry leaves and protect your plants from pests to keep them fresh and healthy.
