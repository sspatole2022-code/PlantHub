Indoor Plant Guidance

Simple tips to keep your indoor plants healthy and happy

🪴

❶ Light

☀️ 🪴
  • Keep indoor plants near a window where they receive indirect sunlight.
  • Avoid strong direct afternoon sunlight, as it can burn the leaves.
  • Rotate the pot occasionally so all sides receive light.

❷ Watering

💧🪴
  • Water only when the top 1–2 inches of soil feels dry.
  • Avoid overwatering because it can cause root rot.
  • Make sure the pot has drainage holes.

❸ Soil

🌱
  • Use well-draining soil suitable for indoor plants.
  • Avoid heavy garden soil because it retains too much water.

❹ Temperature

🌡️
  • Keep indoor plants in a comfortable room temperature.
  • Avoid air conditioners, heaters and cold drafts.

❺ Fertilizer

🌿
  • Apply suitable indoor-plant fertilizer during the growing season.
  • Do not use excessive fertilizer.

❻ Cleaning

🍃
  • Wipe dust from large leaves with a soft damp cloth.
  • Remove yellow, dry or damaged leaves.

❼ Repotting

🪴➡️🪴
  • Repot when the plant becomes root-bound.
  • Choose a pot slightly larger than the existing one.

❽ Common Problems

🔍🍃
  • Yellow leaves: Often caused by overwatering.
  • Drooping leaves: May indicate insufficient water.
  • Brown tips: Can result from dry air or irregular watering.
  • Slow growth: May be caused by insufficient light.
🌿 Healthy Plant = Proper Light + Correct Water + Good Soil + Regular Care
